Base-Building

Showcase of the power grid system in my indie game, Project Blue.
Blocks are split into 3 categories: Consumers, Producers and Power Banks.
The power grid will take the total power generated by producers and distribute it evenly to every consumer that needs power. (Ex: If 10 total power is produces and there's 1 consumer, it will receive the full 10 energy / sec. 3 consumers will only receive 3.33 energy / sec each.) If there are no consumers that need power, the power grid will then begin to charge power banks, using the same logic as consumers.
Consumers will use energy from power banks before using their own energy.
