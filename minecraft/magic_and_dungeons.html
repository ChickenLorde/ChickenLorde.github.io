Core Systems

Spells and Mana System

Mana is the resource that players use to cast spells. There are 4 stats related to mana: Mana, Mana regen, Mana ramping, and exhaustion.
Whenever you cast a spell, it consumes mana and it reduces your mana regen by 10% of the spell's mana cost. Spells also accumulate exhaustion which limits how fast your mana actually replenishes.
The more exhaustion you have, the slower is decreases, so players need to be mindful with their spell usage.
Mana ramping is just how fast your mana regen increases; think like acceleration.



Accessories

Accessories are powerful magic trinkets that can provide a wide variety of benefits from simply increasing your maximum mana capacity to making enemies drop extra loot
You can only equip 2 accessories at first, but you can expand up to 5 over time.
Accessories can be obtained through many different methods, such as enemies, chests, crafting, or dungeons.



Dungeons

Dungeons make up the second half of the content within this plugin. Dungeon parties can contain up to 4 members, but adding more scales the difficulty of the dungeon.
They come in 5 tiers, each tier harder and longer than the last but the higher tiers give handsome rewards.
Each dungeon has a theme, such as fire, ice, standard, etc. and give unique loot. They are also randomly generated, so no dungeon will be the same.
Once you start a dungeon you cannot leave until you either complete it or the entire party dies.



Bosses

Bosses are powerful enemies with multiple, unique attack patterns and drop great loot.
There are three types of bosses: dungeon mini-bosses, dungeon bosses, and elite monsters. Dungeon mini-bosses and dungeon bosses are incredibly powerful and are meant to be fought in dungeons. Elite monsters spawn in the world naturally and aren't as strong.



Explosion Spell

This was just a fun spell I added that can utterly obliterate a village.
It has quite the lengthy and dramatic incantation, so make sure you are safe enough to cast it.
This spell was added as a reference to the character Megumin from the anime Konosuba.
