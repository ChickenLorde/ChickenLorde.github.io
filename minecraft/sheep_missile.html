Navigation System

The sheep is programmed to have "vision" of the terrain around it. Every frame, it shoots out invisible rays in every direction. Each ray tells the sheep information about the block it hit, how far it travelled before hitting one, and how closely aligned it is with the direction the sheep wants to fly in (usually towards the target).
This allows the sheep missile to navigate around terrain while still tracking down its target. The downside is that it struggles to navigate complex terrain with tight turns, so it's best used out in the open for flying around large obstacles like mountains.
