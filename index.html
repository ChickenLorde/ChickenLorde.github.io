  • Project Blue

    Status: Ongoing

    An indie roguelite, survival, base-building video game started in December 2024.

    You are sent off into space in order to extract raw resources to sell to your company, Lone Star Corp. They believe that the best policy for maximum productivity is a lawless environment where employees must fend for themselves.

    Extract resources, defend your outpost, raid rival workers, and most importantly, don't forget to meet the production quota!.

  • Minecraft: Homing Sheep Missile

    Status: Complete

    A fun, experimental project created to test out a dynamic navigation algorithm.

    You can set a target to destroy, and then fire a S.H.E.E.P. (Super Homing Eratic Explosive Projectile) to track it down relentlessly. Running away is pointless as the intelligent navigation brain of the sheep can guide it around obstacles to find its target.

    I eventually reused this algorithm in Project Blue to help with ship navigation.

    🔗 GitHub Repository

  • Minecraft: Magic & Dungeons

    Status: Complete

    Another experimental project to test if I could actually add magic and dungeon generation to Minecraft.

    A complete overhaul of Minecraft that introduces an in-depth mana system, magic, custom enemies, dungeons and more.
    Explore the new magical world to discover rare phenomena and gather knowledge to learn new spells.
    Gather your friends to raid dungeons of varying difficulty and complexity, earning handsome rewards in the process.
    Battle against other mages, gladiator-style, to prove your mastery over the magical combat system!

    I plan on remaking the project to see it to completion in the future.
